Architecture

Why NodeChart Is
Truly Decentralized

No backend. No data brokers. No middlemen between you and the market.

01

Your Browser Connects

NodeChart opens a direct WebSocket to Binance's public market data endpoint. No proxy, no caching layer — raw market data, instantly delivered.

02

Client-Side AI Processes

All indicator calculations, S/R zone detection, and sentiment tinting happen entirely in your browser. Your machine does all the heavy lifting.

03

You Own Your Session

No user data is ever transmitted to any server. Every chart, every session, every setting lives only in your browser. Close the tab and it's gone — true privacy.
